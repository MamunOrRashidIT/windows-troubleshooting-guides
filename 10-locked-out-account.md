# Locked out of your Windows account — what to do (and what not to do)

1. **Stop and wait.** After several wrong passwords, many systems lock the account for a set time (often 15–30 minutes). More attempts can extend the lock — put the keyboard down.
2. **Check the simple things:** Caps Lock on? Keyboard layout changed (EN/DE swap Y and Z and move special characters)? On a laptop, is Num Lock changing letters to numbers?
3. **Try your previous password** if you changed it recently and the PC has been offline — an offline laptop may not know the new password yet. Connect it to the office network or VPN first if you can.
4. **Microsoft account?** Reset the password from another device at the official account reset page, then connect the PC to the internet so it can learn the new password.
5. **Company/domain account?** Only IT / the helpdesk can unlock it — contact them with your username, the exact message on screen, and when it started. That is a normal, quick ticket, not a problem you caused.
6. **Never** use "password unlocker" tools or pay a website to unlock the account — they are a classic route for malware and stolen data.
