# "USB device not recognized" — step by step

1. **Try another port** — and on a desktop, a port on the back of the PC (directly on the mainboard), not the front panel or a hub.
2. **Try another cable.** For phones, drives and headsets, the cable is the most common failure — charge-only cables carry no data at all.
3. **Test the device on another PC.** Two minutes that tells you whether the device or your PC is at fault.
4. **Restart the PC** with the device unplugged, then plug it in after Windows has fully started.
5. **Check Device Manager:** look for the device with a yellow warning sign → right-click → Uninstall device → unplug, wait 10 seconds, plug in again so Windows reinstalls it.
6. **Turn off USB power saving:** Device Manager → USB Root Hub → Properties → Power Management → untick "Allow the computer to turn off this device".
7. **Drives only:** open Disk Management — if the drive appears without a letter, right-click → Change Drive Letter and assign one. Do NOT format it if it holds data.
