# No sound in Microsoft Teams — quick checks

1. **In the meeting:** click the arrow next to the microphone icon and make sure the correct speaker and microphone are selected — after docking/undocking, Teams often keeps the old device.
2. **Check the headset itself:** is it muted on the cable or the earcup? Many headsets have a physical mute button.
3. **Windows settings:** Settings → System → Sound — confirm the same device is chosen for Output and Input, and run the Troubleshoot button if unsure.
4. **Volume mixer:** right-click the speaker icon → Open volume mixer — Teams may be turned down while everything else is loud.
5. **Restart Teams fully:** right-click the Teams icon in the system tray → Quit, then reopen. Minimising is not restarting.
6. **Still nothing?** Unplug the headset and test with the laptop speakers. If they work, the headset or its USB dongle is the problem, not Teams.
